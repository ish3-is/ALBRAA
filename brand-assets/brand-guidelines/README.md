# بداية مسار · A continuous path

هوية شخصية للبراء ياسر العسيري، طالب أمن سيبراني في السنة الثانية بجامعة الملك خالد.

The geometric BA monogram is an open path, representing a beginning and continued learning. It is a personal mark, not an institutional or certification logo.

- Primary ground: Obsidian Navy `#07111F`.
- Accent: Cyan `#46D9E8`; use for actions and the personal mark.
- Text: Soft Ivory `#F5F7FA`; secondary text `#9EADBD`.
- Arabic: Year of the Camel ExtraBold for headlines; ExtraLight at readable sizes for support.
- Latin: Manrope, bundled under the SIL Open Font License in its package.
- Keep at least 15% of the mark width as clear space. Use at least 32px for a full monogram.
- Use the light avatar on light surfaces and the dark version on dark surfaces. Do not stretch or add glow effects.
- LinkedIn cover is exactly 1584 × 396; primary text is on the right, away from the profile-photo overlay. Platforms crop covers differently; inspect the final account preview when the account is available.
- Avatar exports: 1024 × 1024 and 512 × 512, dark and light. All SVG text is outlined for portability.
- No contact details or social URLs are included. Add accounts only after verification.
- Official university and club marks remain separate from the personal monogram. Membership does not imply official endorsement.

## Rebuilding

Run `npm ci` then `npm run brand`. PNG files are rendered directly from vector artwork with loaded fonts, not browser screenshots. The export script is the editable source of the banners. Outline SVG files and the independent monogram can be used in vector editors.

## Certificates

Original photographs are preserved under public/certificates/originals. Display derivatives only correct the paper perspective/crop, preserve all printed content, signatures, barcodes and stamps, and are not recreated documents. Robotics date is intentionally not transcribed because the photograph does not support a confident exact reading. Refer to the original image.
